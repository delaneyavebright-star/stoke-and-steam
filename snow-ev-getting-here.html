Snow days, EV charging and getting here

Where the house is

Stoke and Steam is at 112 Delany Ave, Bright VIC 3741, in Victoria's High Country, north-east of Melbourne.

It's 12 minutes' walk to the edge of town and the nearest cafés (about 19 to the town centre), 5 to the river. There is off-street parking for three cars.

Snow days

The Victorian snow season usually runs from the King's Birthday long weekend in June to early October. From Bright:

  • Mount Hotham is up the Great Alpine Road through Harrietville
  • Falls Creek is over Tawonga Gap and through Mount Beauty
  • Dinner Plain, past Mount Hotham, has a toboggan slope and welcomes dogs all year, on a lead
  • Mount Buffalo has a toboggan and snow play area at Dingo Dell, open when there is enough snow. No dogs: it is a national park

Allow longer in snow or traffic. In the declared snow season you must carry snow chains on the roads to Falls Creek and Mount Hotham, and vehicles need a resort entry permit. Check the resort's website for the season dates before you drive up.

Back at the house there is the drying room set-up in the laundry, two enclosed wood fires and the sauna.

Drying snow gear

A drying room set-up in the laundry: a 10 L/day desiccant dehumidifier, two drying racks and a boot and glove dryer, so snow gear is dry by morning. The laundry has a door, so it shuts while the dehumidifier runs, and a desiccant unit keeps drying on cold mountain nights, when a compressor one struggles.

EV charging

The house has a myenergi EV charger (22 kW three-phase, tethered Type 2 cable) in the front driveway, free for staying guests. The cable is attached to the charger.

How fast your car charges depends on the car as well as the charger.

It's for staying guests only, not a public charge point.

Bookings are handled by Alpine Valley Getaways in Bright. Their page has the calendar, current rates and booking terms.
